# Research Memo: 2026 Solid-State Battery (SSB) Landscape

## Key Industry Players & Progress
- **Toyota**: Targeting commercialization by 2027-2028. Focus is on sulfide-based electrolytes. 2026 is the critical window for pilot production scale-up and vehicle integration testing.
- **Samsung SDI**: Aiming for mass production by 2027. Utilizing a proprietary sulfide-based solid electrolyte. Focus on high-energy density and safety improvements.
- **QuantumScape**: Shifting from A-samples to B-samples. The "B-sample" phase (2025-2026) is the pivotal transition from laboratory prototypes to vehicle-grade cells capable of surviving automotive cycles.

## Technical Breakthroughs (2026 Horizon)
- **Sulfide Electrolytes**: Superior ionic conductivity compared to oxides, allowing faster charging.
- **Ceramic Separators**: Implementation of advanced ceramic layers to mitigate lithium dendrite growth, which has historically caused short circuits.
- **Lab-to-Fab Transition**: The shift from small-scale coin cells to multi-layer pouches and integrated packs.

## Market Risks & Competition
- **LFP & Sodium-Ion**: Low-cost alternatives (LFP) and abundance-based chemistries (Sodium-Ion) are capturing the mass market, potentially pushing SSBs into a "luxury-only" niche.
- **Manufacturing Scalability**: The "valley of death" between prototyping and Giga-scale production remains the primary risk.

## Investment Implications
- The 2026 window is the "Proof of Concept" year for vehicle integration.
- Success in B-sample validation for QuantumScape or Toyota's pilot lines would trigger a massive valuation inflection point.
